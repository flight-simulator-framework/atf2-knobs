    <EPICS device>:parameter.A = LOWER current limit
    <EPICS device>:parameter.B = UPPER current limit
This is the exact spelling/order; neither '.paraemterA' nor A=upper is correct.
Device bases are taken from configured PVs (e.g. SD1R_1, not guessed from the
lattice label). Profile entries now carry lower_limit_pv/upper_limit_pv.
Limits are read per device before response acquisition and again before writes.
Corrector range is their intersection with [-5,+5] A. Skew uses its own supply
limits. NaN, reversed limits or missing members of a configured pair are errors.
The response solver now accepts per-actuator asymmetric bounds.
